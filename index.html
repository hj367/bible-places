    About this atlas

    Every place named in the Protestant Bible, located on the map where modern scholarship puts it. Each place shows how confident researchers are in the identification, the competing sites that have been proposed, and every passage that mentions it.

    Locations and confidence scores come from OpenBible.info's Bible Geocoding Data, which surveys more than seventy atlases, commentaries and dictionaries. Confidence reflects sources published since 2010; the chart on each place traces how that confidence has moved since the 1960s.

    This is a rebuild of the “All Bible Places” Google map, which used OpenBible.info's 2007 data. It keeps those places and adds the 2021 research, region outlines, river courses and scripture text.
